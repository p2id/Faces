Positioning: Dense control surface for frequent review and exception handling.
Palette rationale: Cool gray canvas, white modules, charcoal text, and a reserved amber or blue action accent maintain focus under high information density.
Type rationale: Legible sans-serif with tabular numerals, compact metadata, and consistent label casing.
Layout rules: Split panes on wide screens; summary rail; queue or table as primary surface; visible filters; mobile converts rows into labeled records.
Motion intent: Immediate filter feedback and subtle insertion or removal cues; no parallax or auto-rotation.
Avoided traps: Tiny targets, unbounded tables, excessive borders, simultaneous alerts, hover-only actions, ungrouped density.
