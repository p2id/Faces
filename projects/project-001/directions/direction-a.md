POSITIONING
Calm utility-first planning optimized for quick capture, prioritization, and review.

PALETTE RATIONALE
Warm off-white, graphite text, muted slate secondary text, and blue-green accent support focus while reserving emphasis for actions and priority.

TYPE RATIONALE
Neutral sans serif with tabular numerals for dates and times; medium headings and regular body text improve scanning.

LAYOUT RULES
Use a compact summary, one primary action, prioritized rows, and expandable detail. Stack on narrow screens and use two regions on wide screens with stable action placement.

MOTION INTENT
Use brief fades and positional continuity for add, complete, filter, and reorder states. Support reduced motion and avoid looping movement.

AVOIDED TRAPS
Avoid calendar overload, competing primary actions, false urgency, dense badges, hidden completion states, and color-only priority.
