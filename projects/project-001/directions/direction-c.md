Positioning: Editorial focus; a flexible canvas that makes priorities legible through contrast, rhythm, and selective emphasis.
Palette rationale: Near-white and near-black foundations with one terracotta accent and neutral status tones for controlled distinction.
Type rationale: Sans-serif interface text with a restrained display scale for section landmarks; limit contrast in scale to protect scanability.
Layout rules: Use an asymmetric but predictable grid, wide primary canvas, narrow context rail, featured priority block, and stacked mobile structure that preserves reading order.
Motion intent: Keep the interface mostly static; use reveal and reflow only when they clarify hierarchy, with no parallax, auto-play, or animated decoration.
Avoided traps: Novelty over usability, inconsistent alignment, weak small-screen compression, excessive type contrast, tiny secondary controls, and styling that obscures state.
