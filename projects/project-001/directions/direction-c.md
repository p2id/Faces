Positioning:
Quiet, editorial workspace that turns structured information into focused sequences and readable detail.

Palette rationale:
Light neutral base, slightly warmer reading surface, charcoal text, and muted amber accent for emphasis without urgency.

Type rationale:
Readable sans-serif for controls paired with an optional restrained serif or high-contrast display face for section titles; maintain consistent scale and line length.

Layout rules:
Use a clear reading column with adjacent context only when useful; group content into numbered or titled sections; place secondary actions after the primary reading path; collapse context below content on small screens.

Motion intent:
Use gentle reveal, focus transitions, and anchored section changes; avoid parallax, looping motion, and motion that competes with reading.

Avoided traps:
No ornamental editorial clutter, excessive quotation styling, ambiguous hierarchy, long unbroken text blocks, or hidden state changes.
