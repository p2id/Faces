# Responsive Rules
- Compact: 0–599px.
- Medium: 600–1023px.
- Expanded: 1024px and above.
- Compact: one column, 16px gutters, full-width controls, collapsed navigation, wrapped toolbar, cards or scroll-contained wide data, full-screen dialogs/drawers, minimum 44px targets.
- Medium: two-column layout when readable, compact or collapsible navigation, wrapping toolbar, detail panel up to 640px.
- Expanded: persistent navigation, content/detail split, 1200px maximum content width, aligned heading actions, tables when columns remain legible.
- Global: preserve reading and focus order, avoid unnecessary horizontal scrolling, reflow at 200% zoom, support reduced motion at every width.
