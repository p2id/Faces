# Section C — Responsive Rules

## Breakpoints
- xs: 0–479px
- sm: 480–767px
- md: 768–1023px
- lg: 1024–1279px
- xl: 1280px+

## Rules
- Grid: 4 columns xs/sm, 8 md, 12 lg/xl.
- Gutters: 16px xs/sm, 24px md, 32px lg, 40px xl; content max 1280px.
- Navigation: compact or bottom bar below md; persistent rail at md+.
- Summary cards: one column xs, two sm/md, three lg/xl.
- Lists remain single-column; move secondary metadata below md.
- Tables, if present, become stacked rows below md.
- Dialogs: full-width bottom sheets below sm; centered dialogs at sm+.
- Drawers: full-width below sm; max 420px at sm+, max 480px at lg+.
- Touch targets are at least 44×44px with 8px spacing.
- Essential labels, errors, and values wrap rather than truncate.
- Preserve the primary action; move only secondary actions to overflow.
