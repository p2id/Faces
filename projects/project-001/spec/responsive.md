Breakpoints:
- xs: 0–479px
- sm: 480–767px
- md: 768–1023px
- lg: 1024–1279px
- xl: 1280px and above

Rules:
- xs: single column, compact header, collapsed navigation, stacked rows or table scroll, persistent primary action.
- sm: 16px gutters, wrapped filters, two-column cards only when content remains readable.
- md: optional collapsed navigation, two-column forms for independent fields, dialogs up to 560px.
- lg: optional persistent navigation, content max-width 1200px, three-to-four-column summaries, two-column forms.
- xl: retain content max-width and increase outer whitespace only.
- All widths: controls are at least 44×44px; essential actions do not require hover; preserve reading order.
- Narrow layouts move secondary actions into overflow; destructive actions retain explicit labels.
- Dense data may scroll horizontally; surrounding content must not.
- Reading measure remains approximately 45–85 characters.
