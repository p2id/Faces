SUPPORTING EVIDENCE SUMMARY
- Reusable product experiences consistently benefit from explicit hierarchy, modular composition, semantic status, and predictable navigation.
- Dense information is easier to scan with progressive disclosure, filtering, split-pane inspection, and responsive priority rules.
- Accessibility improves when status combines text, symbols, and color; focus remains visible; motion is optional; and controls retain labels.
- Operational dashboard ranks highest across the weighted criteria and supports the broadest reusable foundation.
- Inventory workspace and Dataset catalog are strong fallbacks because both support structured content, filtering, and scalable narrow layouts.
- Energy monitor offers stronger visual distinction but requires careful chart interpretation.
- Schedule planner and Document review are suitable when sequence or long-form inspection is primary.
- Decorative complexity, hidden actions, forced motion, and modal-heavy interaction are not supported as default patterns.
