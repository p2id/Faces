Project: project-001 | Date: 2026-10-19

Dominant: clear hierarchy, restrained surfaces, compact navigation, progressive disclosure, persistent orientation, and task-focused feedback.
Emerging: adaptive density, contextual summaries, modular cards, quiet status signaling, reversible actions, and preference-aware layouts.
Deprecated: ornamental gradients, dense control chrome, hidden critical actions, excessive modal interruption, decorative motion, and low-contrast secondary text.
Outlier: compressed command-style layouts optimized for speed but limited discoverability.

Visual DNA: neutral foundation, one restrained accent, tonal layering, crisp dividers, moderate radius, purposeful iconography, and content-led composition.
Interactional DNA: predictable focus order, direct manipulation, progressive disclosure, immediate state feedback, reversible actions, and low-friction recovery.
Accessibility DNA: semantic structure, visible focus, keyboard parity, strong contrast, non-color status cues, scalable text, reduced-motion support, and generous hit areas.
Responsive DNA: fluid containers, priority-based content reduction, stacked narrow layouts, preserved primary actions, adaptive navigation, and stable reading rhythm.

Candidate domains:
1. Planning workspace — organizes priorities and related information into an adaptive overview.
2. Learning library — supports discovery, structured reading, progress tracking, and reference retrieval.
3. Service management — coordinates requests, statuses, records, and next actions in a transparent sequence.
4. Resource catalog — enables browsing, comparison, filtering, and detailed inspection of reusable items.
5. Wellness tracker — presents routines, trends, reminders, and summaries without clinical claims.
6. Content review space — supports collecting, annotating, comparing, and approving structured material.
